# ARCHIC COPY — ANTI-SLOP + BRAND VOICE

## Principle

Write for the business and audience, not for a generic AI landing page.

## Hard bans unless the client explicitly uses them

Do not default to vague AI-copy patterns such as “Elevate”, “Unlock”, “Transform”, “Revolutionize”, “Seamless”, “Experience the future”, “Where X meets Y”, “Redefining”, “Crafted for”, “Designed to inspire”, “Discover a world”, “Your journey starts here”, “Excellence redefined”, “Innovation meets”, “More than” or “We don’t just”.

Also reject:

- vague premium/luxury/bespoke/exceptional claims without evidence
- fake scarcity/urgency
- invented customer quotes
- generic social proof
- em-dash-heavy LLM cadence
- three-adjective stacks that could apply to anything

## Headline test

Every headline should pass at least two:

1. names a real outcome, product, category, place or differentiator
2. sounds plausible when spoken by the business
3. distinguishes the client from a competitor
4. supports the next CTA
5. can be substantiated

If it could headline five unrelated companies, rewrite it.

## Prefer

- concrete nouns
- specific verbs
- real locations
- real products/services
- real evidence
- useful constraints
- plain language when it converts better
- personality taken from the business rather than an AI “premium” voice

## CTA rules

Prefer action + outcome. For this project, examples include:

- Ver vehículos
- Consultar una configuración
- Plantear un recorrido
- Solicitar presupuesto
- Ver el caso de Écija

Avoid vague `Explorar`, `Descubrir`, `Saber más` when a more concrete action exists.

## Microcopy

Reduce uncertainty: explain what happens after submitting, what information is required, and whether an action is a request or confirmation. Never imply real-time availability when data is not real-time.
