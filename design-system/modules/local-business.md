# ARCHIC DOMAIN — LOCAL BUSINESS

## Goal

Make the business feel real, trustworthy, easy to contact and unmistakably local.

## Native primitives

- place
- people
- services / catalogue
- contact flow
- local proof
- physical details
- real photography
- location / route

## Avoid

- turning a local/family business into generic SaaS
- fake metrics
- generic feature cards
- stock imagery when real imagery exists
- over-engineered animation
- vague premium language
- hiding useful contact/location information
- replacing useful content with brand theatre

## Prefer

- real photos
- real catalogue/services
- clear contact paths
- local context
- strong mobile-first conversion
- identity from the actual place, vehicles and operation
